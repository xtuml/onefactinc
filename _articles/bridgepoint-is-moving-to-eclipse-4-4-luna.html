--- title: "BridgePoint is moving to Eclipse 4.4 (Luna)" date: 2015-06-14 description: "A 2015 update on the planned move of BridgePoint to the Eclipse Luna platform." permalink: /bridgepoint-is-moving-to-eclipse-4-4-luna/ source_url: "https://onefact.net/bridgepoint-is-moving-to-eclipse-4-4-luna/" ---

One Fact is moving BridgePoint to the Eclipse 4.4 base. The analysis for this task is complete and development is well under way. You can follow the status and discussion about this issue in the xtUML community, in the chat group named Eclipse Luna Migration. The related issue is also captured in the BridgePoint support issue tracking system at issue 36. Join us and provide your input!

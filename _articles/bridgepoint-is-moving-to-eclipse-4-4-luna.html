One Fact is moving BridgePoint to the Eclipse 4.4 base. The analysis for this task is complete and development is well under way. You can follow the status and discussion about this issue in the xtUML.org chat group named Eclipse Luna Migration. The related issues are also captured in the BridgePoint support issue tracking system athttps://support.onefact.net/redmine/issues/36. Join us and provide your input!

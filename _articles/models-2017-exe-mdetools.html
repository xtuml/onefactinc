MODELS continues to be the best modeling conference in the world.  It has a good mix of academic and industrial involvement.  Satellite workshops with special focus precede the larger conference.  xtUML and Shlaer-Mellor development are perennially represented.  This year Cortland Starrett served on the program committee for the EXE (Executable Modeling) workshop and participated as a panelist in a Q&A.  The next day Cort gave the keynote presentation at MDETools.

--- title: "Color Your Model" date: 2020-03-20 description: "Using color and layers in BridgePoint models to communicate information and manage diagram visibility." permalink: /color-your-model/ source_url: "https://xtuml.github.io/onefactnet/color-your-model/" thumbnail: "/assets/images/BP_colorize_ani-3635779.gif" --- BridgePoint lets modelers add color to model elements. Lines can be colored, and shapes can be filled, providing another way to communicate information in a model. A class's color is retained when it is imported into another subsystem. Model elements can also be organized into layers, which can be shown or hidden as needed.

Levi Starrett, President of One Fact Inc, organized an epic 8-day trek to the summit of Mount Kilimanjaro (5895m, 19,341 feet). One Fact partially sponsored the journey in exchange for the summit banner photographs. All seven trekkers successfully reached the summit with the support of their guides, chef, and porters.

Visit Peak Expeditions Tanzania for a detailed day-by-day chronicle of the journey.

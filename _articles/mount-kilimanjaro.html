Levi Starrett, President of One Fact Inc, organized an epic 8-day trek to the summit of Mount Kilimanjaro (5895m, 19,341 feet). 1F partially sponsored the journey in exchange for the sweet banner pics. 7 out of 7 trekkers successfully made the summit thanks to the support of our guides, chef and porters!

--- title: "Saab Coming to West Lafayette" date: 2019-05-08 description: "A 2019 announcement about Saab's planned West Lafayette site near Purdue University and One Fact." permalink: /saab-coming-to-west-lafayette/ source_url: "https://onefact.net/saab-coming-to-west-lafayette/" --- Various divisions within Saab have long been practitioners of executable modeling. On May 8, 2019, Saab announced plans to open a site in the United States near Purdue University and One Fact's headquarters in West Lafayette, Indiana. For additional context, the original article linked to these announcements and reports: - [Saab press release](https://saab.com/news-and-media/2019-05/saab-announces-new-u.s.-site-for-advanced-manufacturing-and-production/) - [Saab: The Journey to West Lafayette](https://saab.com/region/usa/about-saab/the-journey-to-west-lafayette/) - [Purdue University press release](https://www.purdue.edu/newsroom/releases/2019/Q2/saabs-global-defense-and-security-company-lands-in-indiana,-to-open-site-near-purdue-university.html) - [West Lafayette local reporting](https://www.wlfi.com/content/news/Saab-and-Purdue-announce-plans-to-open-site-near-University-509638591.html) *Historical note: This article records the 2019 announcement and its expectations at that time; it is not a statement of the facility's current operations.*

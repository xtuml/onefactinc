xtUML Days UK flew directly into MODELS EU. One Fact and the BridgePoint engineers have participated in the MODELS conference in various capacities the last several years. This year the focus was on the MDE Tools workshop. Participants included Professor Juergen Dingel of Queens University, Bran Selic, Francis Bordeleau and Ed Seidewitz.
The conference venue was in Munich. The social gatherings were done German style with a free flow of Bavarian beverages.

--- title: "Issue Tracking with Redmine" date: 2016-02-18 description: "A historical note on the xtUML team's use of Redmine for customer service requests and community bug tracking." permalink: /issue-tracking-with-redmine/ source_url: "https://xtuml.github.io/onefactnet/issue-tracking-with-redmine/" --- In 2016, the xtUML team used Redmine for issue tracking. When BridgePoint was open-sourced, the team evaluated freely available, open issue trackers and selected Redmine. The article described two principal uses: - One Fact customers could report service requests. - The wider xtUML community could report BridgePoint bugs, participate in their resolution, and track progress. The original post also noted Redmine's project-management features and linked to the team's issue tracker. The original link was [support.onefact.net](https://support.onefact.net/). *Historical note: this article reflects the team's tool choice in February 2016. It is not a statement about the current issue-tracking system or current support process.*

--- title: "Model-Based Testing of Self-Driving Cars" date: 2017-08-01 description: "How model-based test engineering can address the enormous sensor-input space in autonomous-vehicle testing." permalink: /model-based-testing-self-driving-cars/ source_url: "https://xtuml.github.io/onefactnet/model-based-testing-self-driving-cars/" --- Self-driving cars pose significant testing challenges. The quantity and magnitude of sensor inputs create an enormous test-vector space, making it impractical for traditional manual testing to provide adequate coverage. The article proposed model-based techniques to manage this complexity. Abstraction and automation can increase test-engineering productivity by supporting the generation of: - Test cases - Test data - Predicted system behavior The described strategy included sensor test generation, automated navigation testing, and turbulence-induced testing. The original paper presented a case study and summarized conclusions from applying these techniques. The original paper is available as [Self-Driving Car Model Testing (PDF)](https://xtuml.github.io/onefactnet/wp-content/uploads/2017/08/SeldModelTest.pdf). *Historical note: this article and paper date from August 2017. They describe an earlier stage of autonomous-vehicle testing; they should be read as a case study rather than a survey of current industry practice.*

Congratulations to Levi Starrett upon his promotion to Chief Software Architect.

Levi is responsible for the structure and technology of BridgePoint.  And will…

  • Research and choose technologies for implementation (such as Java, EMF, Eclipse, Xtext, Python, M2M).
  • Define and maintain interfaces between modules in the tooling.
  • Freshen the technology in a manner that is “Incremental, Always Advancing, Never Broken”.
  • Serve as lieutenant steward of the xtUML modeling methodology.
  • Champion BridgePoint engineering processes.
